Why this matters

Flooding affects the whole village.

Major rainfall can combine surface water, drainage constraints and the Bloxham Brook to create serious flooding. Understanding where water comes from — and how proposed measures work together — is central to reducing future risk.

This is an independent community information site. It does not replace official flood warnings or emergency advice.
