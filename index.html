Why this matters

Storm Bert changed the conversation in Bloxham.

Between 22 and 24 November 2024, intense rainfall caused serious surface-water and river flooding across the village. The official Section 19 investigation recorded up to 35 properties as reported flooded, but community information indicates the wider number of properties affected was probably nearer 65; a precise total is not known. At least five vehicles were also badly affected.

Flooding came from several interacting sources: rapid runoff from higher ground, overloaded or restricted drainage, blocked gullies and drains, and the Bloxham Brook exceeding channel and bridge capacity. This site therefore tracks both engineered drainage work and Natural Flood Management across the wider catchment.

The figure of around 65 affected properties is a community estimate and is broader than the number formally recorded in the Section 19 report. This is an independent community information site and does not replace official flood warnings or emergency advice.
