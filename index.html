The Bloxham story

A village shaped by several connected flood routes.

Bloxham’s flood risk is not caused by one single source. Surface water from surrounding land, highway and drainage systems, local topography and the Bloxham Brook can interact during prolonged or intense rainfall.

After the November 2024 flooding, the Parish Council Flooding Working Group reported that at least 25 properties had been affected, with a rough estimate of damage of around £1.5 million. Since then, work has included the Oxfordshire County Council Section 19 investigation, drainage reviews, landowner discussions and Natural Flood Management studies.

This is an independent community information site. It does not replace official flood warnings or emergency advice.
