Latest updates · Bloxham, Oxfordshire

News & Progress

A dated record of practical work, investigations and milestones in Bloxham's flood-resilience programme. We will update this page as projects develop.

23 September 2026

Workhouse Lane — partners identify further practical measures

A meeting was held today to discuss ways of reducing flood events in the Workhouse Lane area. Representatives attended from Bloxham School, Bloxham Parish Council, Oxfordshire County Council, affected residents and the Flooding Working Group.

Several new practical measures were identified for further action. The focus is on holding back water and slowing the flow into Workhouse Lane, alongside the continuing investigation of drainage solutions in this part of the village. Further updates will be published as the measures are developed and progressed.

23 September 2026

Stop Bloxham Flooding website launched

The new Stop Bloxham Flooding website is now live. It brings together information about why Bloxham floods, current projects, Natural Flood Management, practical advice for residents, evidence and reports, flood photographs and video, and information for farmers and landowners.

Its central message is simple: Keep drains clear. Slow the flow. The website will also provide a permanent home for future news and progress reports.

June 2026

Natural Flood Management feasibility work published

The Bloxham Natural Flood Management feasibility work by Thames21, supported by Oxfordshire County Council which funded the work, was published. It assesses opportunities to slow and temporarily store runoff in the wider catchment and provides evidence to help guide discussions with participating landowners and future project design.

The study identified about 3,535 m³ of potential temporary storage across the proposals studied. Natural Flood Management is one part of the response and needs to work alongside effective drainage within the village.

2026 programme

The Slade — Natural Flood Management work progressing

The Slade is one of the current areas of practical Natural Flood Management activity, including leaky woody dams and measures intended to spread and slow flows. The objective is to delay some runoff during periods of heavy rain and reduce the speed at which water moves downstream.

Since Storm Bert · November 2024

From flood investigation to practical action

Following the serious flooding during Storm Bert, work has included investigation of drainage problems, clearance activity, partnership discussions, surveys and development of possible flood-reduction measures. The programme now combines work on village drainage with investigation of opportunities to slow runoff in the surrounding catchment.
