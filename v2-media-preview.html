Community evidence

Flooding in Bloxham

We are building a visual record of what actually happens during severe rainfall. These photographs and video clips help show the depth, speed and routes of floodwater through the village.

Curated media record. The Google Drive archive has been reviewed and reduced to a publication shortlist rather than displaying every near-duplicate. The full source archive remains intact. One photograph includes a clearly visible resident; permission for publication has been confirmed.

1. Storm Bert — 24 November 2024

The strongest material shows the flood as it developed: water spreading across the road, entering the property and moving through ground-floor rooms. These images and clips are particularly valuable because they show both depth and movement, not simply the aftermath.

2. Immediate aftermath

After the water receded

20241126_140818.jpg and 20241128_123158.jpg show the condition left behind and help explain why flood recovery continues long after the rainfall ends.

Why this matters

The site will use only enough interior material to demonstrate impact. Detailed insurance-style photographs and repetitive room-by-room evidence will remain in the underlying archive rather than being published routinely.

4. Repair & reinstatement

Rebuilding

Editorial approach

The finished public gallery will use roughly 9–10 photographs and 3 videos from this Drive collection. Vehicle registrations and any unnecessary personal details will be obscured before publication where needed.

Archive categories: Flood Event — Storm Bert · Immediate Aftermath · Damage & Recovery · Repairs & Reinstatement. This makes the collection useful as evidence rather than simply a photo dump.
