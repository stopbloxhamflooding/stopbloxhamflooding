Flooding in Bloxham

We are building a visual record of what actually happens during severe rainfall. These photographs and video clips help show the depth, speed and routes of floodwater through the village.

Media preview. I have reviewed the material supplied so far and selected a first set for the website. More photographs and videos can be added as they arrive. Locations and dates will only be stated where we can verify them.

Flooding at night

One of the clearest images in the current collection, showing floodwater spreading across the road and towards property.

Fast-moving street runoff — video

The supplied video sequence shows water moving along the road rather than simply standing in place — useful evidence for understanding flow routes.

Road and field runoff — video

Another useful clip from the sequence, retained because it helps show how water is arriving and moving through the village during heavy rainfall.

More material already reviewed

The current collection also includes the flooded recreation ground/play area, flooded roads, the culvert/channel and further street-flow videos. These will form the fuller gallery as captions and locations are confirmed.
