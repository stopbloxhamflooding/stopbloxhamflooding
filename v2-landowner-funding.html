Why it matters

Could winter water become an asset?

Temporary NFM storage holds stormwater and releases it gradually. In suitable places, separate or complementary farm water-storage measures may also help retain winter rainfall for use during dry periods.

For Bloxham, the opportunity is to explore measures that reduce the peak flow reaching the village while remaining practical for the farm. In some circumstances, better winter-water management may also strengthen farm resilience during dry summers.

Not every option will suit every holding. Permanent ponds, reservoirs and abstraction can have different planning, licensing and funding requirements from temporary NFM storage.
